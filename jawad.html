FAQ

Questions property owners ask.

Do I need to repair my house before selling?

No. Properties can be evaluated in their current condition. Let us know what needs attention and we'll take that into consideration when reviewing the property.

Do I have to accept the offer?

No. Requesting an evaluation does not obligate you to sell. You can review the offer and decide whether it makes sense for your situation.

What types of properties do you buy?

We primarily look at residential properties throughout Texas, including single-family homes, rentals, townhomes, inherited properties and homes that need repairs.

How quickly can a property close?

Timing depends on the property and circumstances. If an agreement is reached, we'll discuss a closing timeline that works for both sides.

Is there any obligation when I submit the form?

No. The form simply starts a conversation about your property. You remain free to decide whether you want to move forward.
